## Findings

### Blocking

1. `Tracker/Tracker.Server/Components/Pages/Diagnostics.razor.cs` queued playback tick can still mutate selection after `StopPlayback()` or mode switch.
   `RunPlaybackAsync` checks cancellation before `Task.Delay`, but the queued `InvokeAsync` callback did not re-check `cancellationToken.IsCancellationRequested` or the active playback mode. A stale callback could therefore call `SelectEntryByIndex` or reset to first after the user stopped playback, switched mode, reloaded, or scrubbed. This violates the requirement that Stop preserves the current selection.

### Non-blocking

- `Tracker/Tracker.Server/Design/tasks-status.md` and `Tracker/Tracker.Server/Design/phases-status.md` still show RAW-VISION-012 as in-progress while this finding remains open. Sync before task closure.
- The 2 second maximum clamp was removed and tests cover long timestamp deltas, fast-forward quarter interval, fast-forward minimum interval, and reset-to-first behavior.
